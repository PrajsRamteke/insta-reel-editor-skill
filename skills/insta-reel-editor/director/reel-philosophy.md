# Reel Philosophy

A reel is a contract renewed every 3 seconds. The viewer's thumb is already moving. Every cut, word
and graphic either earns the next 3 seconds or loses them. Motion design here is not decoration:
it is **attention management at phone distance, sound on or off**.

## The three pillars

### 1. Retention intent: what keeps them for the next 3 seconds?

Before choosing a font or an ease, decide what the viewer should **feel** and **do** in each stretch.

| Stretch | Viewer state you want | Tools |
|---------|-----------------------|-------|
| 0–1 s | "Wait, what?" (stop scrolling) | Hook title on frame 0, a bold claim, motion or a face close-up |
| 1–3 s | "This is for me" | Name the pain or promise; context in one line |
| Body | "I'm learning something / I need the next one" | One idea per beat, list counters, open loops, visual change every 3–5 s |
| Payoff | "Oh, that's useful" | The clearest graphic of the reel lands here |
| End | "Save it / send it / watch again" | CTA (save or send beats follow), or a line that loops into the opening |

Instagram itself says its top signals are **watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach** (Mosseri,
Jan 2025). Sends matter most for reaching non-followers. Edit for completion and shareability, not
for prettiness.

### 2. Visual narrative: the micro-story

Every reel, even 12 seconds of tips, has **Hook → Context → Value beats → Payoff → CTA/Loop**.
Every graphic belongs to one of those beats. A graphic that belongs to none is noise. The duration
shares (from [hook-and-retention.md](hook-and-retention.md)):

| Part | Share of runtime | Notes |
|------|------------------|-------|
| Hook | 1–3 s | Spoken and written at the same time |
| Context | 0–10% | Often fused with the hook |
| Value beats | 70–80% | One idea per beat; each beat gets at most one hero graphic |
| Payoff | 10–20% | The best line and the best visual |
| CTA / loop | 0–2 s | Short; a loop beats a "follow for more" plea |

### 3. Production craft: does it look and sound pro on a phone?

Clean, loud-enough voice. Cuts with no pops or clipped words. Captions you can read at arm's length
in sunlight. Motion with weight and easing. A frame that respects Instagram's interface. Craft is
invisible when it is right and screams when it is wrong.

## The three layers

A flat reel is missing a layer. A cluttered reel has too much in one layer.

| Layer | What | Amplitude | Rules |
|-------|------|-----------|-------|
| **A-roll** (primary) | The speaker, demo or footage | 100% | Face clear of UI zones; punch-ins hide jump cuts |
| **Information** | Captions plus one graphic at a time | Readable | Captions are never covered; graphics land on words |
| **Ambient** | Drift zoom, legibility scrims, music bed, progress bar | 10–20% | Never competes; nobody should consciously notice it |

## The attention budget

- **One hero at a time.** At any moment: captions plus at most one graphic. Chapter chips and
  lower-thirds are small "secondary" elements and may coexist with captions, but not with another
  hero graphic.
- **One new thing at a time.** Two simultaneous reveals means the eye catches neither. Stagger,
  or sequence.
- **Never move the camera while a graphic enters.** Punch-ins happen on cuts; graphics land on words.
  When both fall in the same 0.3 s, move the graphic.
- **Ambient doesn't count** against the budget, but stays below 20% of the energy of the primary motion.

## Restraint and authenticity

Instagram's head said in early 2026 that the most trustworthy content now "looks the least
produced". Instagram also deprioritizes reels that are **majority text**, watermarked by other apps,
bordered, muted or reposted. So:

- Let the person be the star. Graphics clarify, they don't replace the speaker.
- A graphic must make the idea **easier to understand or remember** than the spoken words alone.
  If it only repeats the caption, cut it.
- Keep most frames where the face is visible. Full-frame cover cards are for short, high-value moments
  (myth vs fact, a key diagram), typically under 25% of the runtime.
- Your own logo or handle is fine. Never leave TikTok or CapCut watermarks or template marks.
- Educational ≠ text-heavy. Explain with the voice and **anchor** with the graphic (a number, a list
  counter, a verdict).

## Decision quick-path

```
1. ARCHETYPE   → reel-archetypes.md (structure, pace, density, theme)
2. HOOK        → strongest line in the transcript; can it be the first words? (cold open)
3. BEATS       → content-analysis.md → beats.md (one idea per beat, triggers marked)
4. PERSONALITY → motion-personality.md (one per reel; matches the brand + emotion)
5. GRAPHICS    → only beats with a trigger; land on the payoff word; one hero at a time
6. CADENCE     → visual change every 3–5 s (cut / punch-in / graphic / b-roll); nothing static > 8 s
7. ENDING      → CTA to save/send, or a loop line
8. VERIFY      → watch it as a stranger with the sound off: do you get it, and would you send it?
```

## Evaluation before delivery

| Check | Question |
|-------|----------|
| Hook | Is the hook readable on frame 0, and does the spoken line start within 0.5 s? |
| Purpose | Can you name the beat and the trigger for every graphic? |
| Sound off | Can you follow the reel muted? |
| Sound on | Is the voice clear, the music under it, and no pops? |
| Repetition | Would it be fine on a third loop? |
| Respect | Is there no text in UI zones and nothing over the eyes or mouth? |
| Identity | Is there one personality and one theme, applied consistently? |
